# Ece Ceyla Demir

Electrical and Electronics Engineering student at Middle East Technical University (METU), with a research focus on **analog/mixed-signal integrated circuits, RFICs, SiGe semiconductor devices, and circuit-to-layout verification**.

My work bridges transistor-level circuit characterization, electromagnetic modeling, and embedded electronics. During my 2026 research internships, I worked on open-source RFIC simulation workflows at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) and underwater acoustic telemetry systems at Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC).

## Selected research and engineering projects

- **[IHP SG13G2 RFIC Characterization](https://github.com/ececeyla/ihp-sg13g2-rfic-characterization)** — SiGe HBT DC/AC/RF and noise analyses; S-parameter stability metrics; KLayout, openEMS, custom transmission lines, three-port T-junctions, and single-device DRC/LVS. Includes a historical internship report and clearly marked reconstructed post-processing scripts.
- **[Underwater Acoustic Telemetry](https://github.com/ececeyla/underwater-acoustic-telemetry)** — ESP32-S3-based surface and underwater node communication workflow, acoustic modem interfacing, GNSS, LTE-M/NB-IoT and monitoring interfaces.
